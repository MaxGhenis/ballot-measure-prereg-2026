## Common rules for this lane

- Context: a neutral research pre-registration of an event study. On November 3, 2026, do prediction-market moves in the odds of state and local ballot measures move each state's municipal bond prices? Nothing here is investment advice, and no fund or product is involved. Do not contact anyone, post, or submit anything. Do not create accounts.
- Use primary sources only: secretary of state and election office pages, the official voter guide, legislative fiscal offices (for example LAO, OFM, REC, legislative fiscal notes), the measure's text, and fund sponsors' own product pages. Secondary sources such as Ballotpedia or news can help you find a primary source. Never cite one as the source, and mark any fact you could not confirm from a primary source UNVERIFIED.
- Every fact gets the source URL, the retrieval date (2026-10-16 or later), and a short verbatim quote of 25 words or fewer that supports it.
- Do not try to reach kalshi.com or clob.polymarket.com. This network blocks them, and you do not need them.
- Be complete within your scope. If you cap coverage, say exactly what you left out.
- Output: one markdown file with the tables specified below, then a list of gaps and UNVERIFIED items.
