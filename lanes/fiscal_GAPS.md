# Lane brief: November 3, 2026 measures that Kalshi lists in one-off series (gap fill)

Read `lanes/_common.md` first; its rules apply. This network blocks Kalshi, so work only from official sources. Each series below is a Kalshi market family whose markets were not captured. Settle whether the measure behind it is on the November 3, 2026 ballot, and if so, what it is.

Series: KXBAYAREATRANSIT ("Bay Area Regional Transit Measure"), KXSFBALLOTMEASURES ("Which San Francisco ballot measures will pass?"), KXMICONSTITUTION ("Michigan constitutional convention question"), KXMARENTCONTROL ("Will the Massachusetts rent control initiative pass?"), KXSQ832 ("Oklahoma SQ 832 Minimum Wage Initiative"), KXKSAMEND ("Kansas Supreme Court Election Method Amendment"), KXREPRODUCTIVEMEASURE and KXREPRODUCTIVEMEASURES ("Which reproductive rights measures will pass in 2026?"), KXHONOLULUQ4 ("Honolulu Charter Question 4"), KXCAVOTERID ("Will the California Voter ID initiative pass?").

For each:

1. **Ballot status.** Is a measure matching it on the November 3, 2026 ballot (statewide or local)? Give the official designation and title from the election office: county or city for local measures, secretary of state for statewide. If it was decided at an earlier election, or never qualified, say which.
2. **Fiscal estimate.** For each measure on the November 3, 2026 ballot that changes taxes, fees, revenue, borrowing or required spending, give the official fiscal estimate. That means the annual change in state and local revenue, recurring or one-time, the first fiscal year, and bond principal and debt service, with the source and a verbatim quote of 25 words or fewer. The sources are the county or city controller's or legislative analyst's statement, the regional agency's official analysis, or the state fiscal note. If none is published, say "none published".
3. **San Francisco.** List every local measure on San Francisco's November 3, 2026 ballot, with its letter, title and the Controller's fiscal statement.
4. **Bay Area.** For the regional transit measure, list the counties that vote on it, the tax rate and term, and the official revenue estimate.
5. **Michigan.** Give the constitutional convention question's official wording and any other statewide Michigan proposals on the November 3 ballot.

**Output tables:** (a) series → official measure(s) and ballot status; (b) fiscal estimates; (c) San Francisco measures; (d) gaps and UNVERIFIED items.
