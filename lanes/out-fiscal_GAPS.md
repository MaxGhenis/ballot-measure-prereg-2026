## 4. Bay Area (RTM)
- **Counties that vote:** Alameda, Contra Costa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara.
- **Rate and term:** 0.5% in the four other counties and 1% in San Francisco, for 14 years. The April 1, 2027 to April 1, 2041 dates are SECONDARY ([Eno](https://enotrans.org/article/transportation-at-the-ballot-box-2026-highlight-connect-bay-area-transit/)).
- **Revenue:** about $980,000,000 a year (ballot question, [index]). MTC/Sperry put it at about $978M in FY27. Muni's share of the SF proceeds is about $155M a year (SFCTA/SFMTA, [index]).
- **Passage threshold:** simple majority (SECONDARY, LWV).
