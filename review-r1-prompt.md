# Adversarial review, round 1: pre-registration of the November 3, 2026 ballot-measure study (prereg-nov2026/)

You are an independent reviewer. The sandbox is read-only and may have no shell; put everything in your final message. Do not contact anyone, post, or submit anything. The commit under review is `d497c6c` of the repository one level up.

**What it is.** `REGISTRATION.md` pre-registers a test of whether ballot-measure passage odds on Kalshi and Polymarket move state municipal bond spreads on election night (November 4–6) and later count days, pooled across measures and scaled by each measure's official fiscal estimate. `RUNBOOK.md` gives the steps; it will be frozen as a git commit with sha256 sums (`scripts/freeze.sh`) before October 30. The October study it builds on is `../memo.md`. Max's specification for it, verbatim: "Enumerate every state and local measure on the Nov 3 ballot with a Kalshi or Polymarket market… check liquidity and the official APIs. Map each measure to a testable asset: a state-specific muni fund or ETF, ideally with intraday prices. Record the gaps. Pre-specify: event windows (election night and the count days that follow; California counts slowly); the per-measure test and a POOLED test that scales each measure's price move by its official fiscal estimate (bp per $ billion of revenue change, with PolicyEngine where it covers the measure); circular-shift standard errors as in the memo; a power analysis giving the detection floor for the pooled estimate; and the outcomes that count as confirming or refuting a policy effect."
